# Example prompts for the TikTok Ads MCP server

These work as written in Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex, Gemini CLI and the other clients listed in the README. Anything that changes the account comes back as an approval first.

## Read and report

- With Sprites, which TikTok ads have the lowest CPA this month?
- Use Sprites to find TikTok ad groups spending with no conversions in 7 days.
- With Sprites, find TikTok hook ideas in what our customers complain about on Reddit.
- Use Sprites to compare our Spark ads against in-feed ads on CPA last 30 days.

## Change, create and launch

- Ask Sprites for 6 new TikTok hooks based on our top performer.
- Have Sprites launch a TikTok campaign for the summer bundle at $150/day.
- Ask Sprites to pause the TikTok ads whose CPA is double the account average.
- With Sprites, write three 15-second scripts in the style of our best ad.
- Have Sprites raise the budget on our best TikTok ad group by $40/day.

## Recurring

- Use Sprites to check TikTok creative fatigue every morning and tell me only when it matters.

## Tips

- Name the campaign, ad set or page in full. Sprites receives only the request, not the earlier chat.
- Say the period ("last 30 days", "this quarter"); your client passes today's date so it resolves correctly.
- Working on several businesses? Say "switch to <business name>" first (`choose_business`).
- To compare channels, ask about them in one request, for example "compare Google and Meta CPA last month".
